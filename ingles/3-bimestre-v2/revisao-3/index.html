Página 3 de 12 • 7 min

Should = deveria

Conselho: Subject + should + verbo na forma base.
You should study. = Você deveria estudar.
She should charge her phone. = Ela deveria carregar o celular dela.
⚠️ Nunca use to depois de should: should to study.

Página 4 de 12 • 7 min

Shouldn't = não deveria

Shouldn't dá um conselho negativo: You shouldn't share personal information. = Você não deveria compartilhar informações pessoais.
Should I...? é usado para perguntar se algo é aconselhável: Should I click on this link?
I got a strange link. (Recebi um link estranho.)
You shouldn't click on it.
What should I do?
You should check the website first.
Página 5 de 12 • 7 min

Internet safety: entenda o contexto

Agora as traduções começam a diminuir. Guarde: safe = seguro, dangerous = perigoso, personal information = informações pessoais, stranger = desconhecido, fake = falso.

Lucy: I received a message from a stranger. It says: “Click here and win a new phone!”

Mia: You shouldn't click on the link. You should check if the website is safe. You shouldn't share your address or password.

Página 6 de 12 • 8 min

Possessive adjectives: my, your, his...

Eles vêm antes de um substantivo.
I → my phone • you → your tablet • he → his charger • she → her laptop • we → our webcam • they → their headphones.
Pergunte: vem um objeto depois? Se sim, provavelmente você precisa de my/your/his/her/our/their.
Página 7 de 12 • 8 min

Possessive pronouns: mine, yours, hers...

Eles substituem o objeto e aparecem sozinhos.
This is my tablet. → The tablet is mine.
That is her phone. → The phone is hers.
my → mine | your → yours | his → his | her → hers | our → ours | their → theirs

Página 8 de 12 • 7 min

Whose = de quem?

Whose phone is this? = De quem é este celular?
It's Ana's. / It's hers.

Whose headphones are these? = De quem são estes fones?
They're mine.

Observe que whose pergunta sobre posse. Agora você já não precisa de tradução para phone, tablet, charger, share, file.

Emma's online day

Emma uses technology every day. In the morning, she checks her email. After school, she sometimes streams movies and sends text messages to her friends. She never shares her password. Yesterday, she saw a strange ad, but she didn't click on it. Her brother said, “You should report suspicious posts.” Emma thinks technology is useful, but people should use it safely.

Social media: opinião sem perder o foco da prova

No livro, redes sociais aparecem como contexto. Algumas palavras menos usuais: filters = filtros, compare = comparar, mental health = saúde mental, experts = especialistas. Use esse tema para treinar o conteúdo oficial.

Social media can be useful. We can talk to friends and share ideas. However, people sometimes compare themselves with photos that use filters. Experts say we should remember that online photos do not always show real life.
